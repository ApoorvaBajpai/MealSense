# MealSense: Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) Framework

Framework format:  
**When [Situation / Context], I want to [Motivation / Action], so I can [Expected Outcome / Value].**

---

## 1. Student Job Stories

### Core Job: Meal Attendance Intention
> **When** I decide whether I am eating an upcoming meal at the hostel mess,  
> **I want to** submit my decision in a single tap without filling out cumbersome forms,  
> **So I can** ensure the kitchen cooks the right amount and prevents food from being thrown away.

### Job 2: Multi-Day Absence
> **When** I am going home for the weekend or traveling for a conference,  
> **I want to** activate an "Away Mode" for the date range in one action,  
> **So I can** automatically inform the kitchen for all meals during my absence without daily manual toggles.

### Job 3: Personal Impact Validation
> **When** I take the time to respond to daily meal polls,  
> **I want to** see transparent data on how my individual participation saved food and reduced campus waste,  
> **So I can** feel that my small daily action creates real environmental and institutional value.

---

## 2. Kitchen Staff Job Stories

### Core Job: Production Batch Sizing
> **When** it is time to start cooking cauldrons for the upcoming meal (10:30 AM for Lunch / 5:30 PM for Dinner),  
> **I want to** see an unambiguous, decision-ready recommended preparation quantity with an explicit safety buffer,  
> **So I can** cook enough to serve every student who arrives without running short or producing excess unserved waste.

### Job 2: Operational Adjustment with Accountability
> **When** I possess local contextual knowledge (e.g., heavy rain outside or a departmental cricket match),  
> **I want to** adjust the recommended quantity and record my specific operational reason,  
> **So I can** apply culinary judgment while preserving an audit trail that helps the system learn.

### Job 3: Post-Meal Outcome Audit
> **When** a meal service concludes,  
> **I want to** quickly record actual attendees, unserved tray food, and plate scrapings in under 60 seconds,  
> **So I can** fulfill mess logging requirements, track daily waste trends, and verify zero shortages occurred.

---

## 3. Institutional Administrator / Warden Job Stories

### Core Job: Operational Progress Oversight
> **When** evaluating mess contractor performance and dining operations,  
> **I want to** monitor the North Star metric (Avoidable Food Waste per Meal Served) alongside student shortage rates,  
> **So I can** confirm that waste is declining without compromising dining quality or student welfare.

### Job 2: Financial & Baseline Justification
> **When** defending dining budgets or presenting sustainability reports to university trustees,  
> **I want to** compare current metrics against a rigorous, defensible pre-implementation baseline,  
> **So I can** substantiate cost savings and waste diversion figures with audited empirical evidence.

### Job 3: Operational Problem Diagnosis
> **When** anomalies occur (such as sudden waste spikes or low student engagement),  
> **I want to** receive rule-based deterministic operational insights highlighting specific meal types or menus,  
> **So I can** take targeted action (such as adjusting vendor recipes or revising cutoff deadlines) before problems recur.
